Privacy

Your games stay on your PC.

This notice covers this website and the Rift Assistant desktop app for Windows. Plain words, no surprises. Last updated 12 October 2026.

Who runs this

Rift Assistant is an independent project run by a single developer based in New Zealand. A verified contact address will be published on this page before any download or paid plan is offered. Until then this site is a preview of a private prototype: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It has no forms, accounts, analytics, advertising, cookies or browser storage, and it loads only its own styles, logo and bundled fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes ordinary connection details such as your IP address to serve the pages and describes that in the GitHub privacy statement. We do not receive visitor-level logs.

The desktop app

  • Recordings and clipsare made on your PC and saved in folders you choose. Nothing is uploaded by the app. Whole-screen capture can include other apps and pop-ups, so review the capture choice before turning recording on. Storage limits can remove older recordings; favourites are kept.
  • Match recordsare downloaded from Riot's official API and stored locally, compressed. They include the other nine players' in-game statistics as Riot provides them. They are not encrypted; a cloud-synced folder could copy them off your PC.
  • Champion and item artworkis fetched from Riot's public Data Dragon service without any key or player identifier. Riot sees ordinary connection details.
  • Your League clientis read locally for champion select and live-game information that the client already shows you. The app never reads game memory, never injects anything and never automates play.
  • Settings and review notesstay in the app's own data folder next to the program.

The app does not send crash reports, usage statistics or telemetry anywhere.

What we don't do

No selling or sharing of personal information. No advertising. No tracking across sites. No attempt to identify players who have hidden their names.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or raise a concern, once the contact address above is published. You can also contact New Zealand's Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, a paid plan or an online service, this notice will be updated first and the date above will change. Nothing on this page describes a service that does not exist yet.
